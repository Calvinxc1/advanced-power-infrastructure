# Advanced Power Infrastructure

Advanced Power Infrastructure expands Factorio 2.1 + Space Age power progression with additional tiers for boilers, steam engines, steam turbines, heat exchangers, heat pipes, nuclear reactors, fusion power, solar panels, and accumulators.

The mod gives power generation a broader progression curve from early steam through late-game nuclear, fusion, solar, and storage upgrades. Space Age materials and science packs are used to place higher tiers naturally across Nauvis, Fulgora, Vulcanus, Aquilo, and the promethium endgame.

Advanced Power Infrastructure focuses only on generation and storage. Electric grid infrastructure is handled by the optional Advanced Energy Grid companion mod, and fluid pipe/pump infrastructure is handled by the optional Advanced Fluid Infrastructure companion mod. Both companions are optional; this mod remains loadable on its own with Space Age.

This is an early 0.2.x public release. The mod loads and core progression is implemented, but balance values, long-running save upgrade paths, and broad compatibility with other power mods should still be treated as experimental.
